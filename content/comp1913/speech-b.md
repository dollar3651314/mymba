## 第 10 页 · 增长战略总览
- "Thank you. I will explain our options and our plan." —— 谢谢。我来讲我们的选项和计划。
- "This slide shows our growth strategy in one picture." —— 这页用一张图展示我们的增长战略。
- "It has three parts: buy wide, build premium, and keep a backup." —— 它有三部分：广泛收购、建设高端、留好后备。
- "The top row is buying. We buy one company a year." —— 第一行是收购：每年买一家公司。
- "l'Acre in France in Year 1, S. Bound in Japan and India in Year 2, and Planeta in Brazil in Year 3." —— 第 1 年法国的 l'Acre，第 2 年日本和印度的 S. Bound，第 3 年巴西的 Planeta。
- "Together, they add about 1.25 billion dollars of sales." —— 三家加起来，增加约 12.5 亿美元的销售额。
- "The middle row is building. We build three egg-product factories at 25 million dollars each, and spend 10 million on cage-free barns." —— 第二行是建设：建三座蛋制品工厂，每座 2,500 万美元，另花 1,000 万美元建散养鸡舍。
- "We build the second and third factories only after customers sign contracts." —— 第二、第三座工厂要等客户签了合同才建。
- "The bottom row is our backup: Free Range Roost in Thailand." —— 第三行是后备：泰国的 Free Range Roost。
- "By Year 3, we are in four world regions, and a third of our sales come from outside North America." —— 到第 3 年，我们进入全球四个地区，三分之一的销售额来自北美以外。

## 第 11 页 · 我们否定的路线
- "Before we chose this plan, we tested four other routes. Each one fails at least one test." —— 选这个计划之前，我们检验了另外四条路线，每条至少有一项不过关。
- "Build only: we would need 22 new factories, and premium sales would have to triple. And we would have no global presence." —— 只自建：需要 22 座新工厂，高端蛋销售额要翻三倍，而且没有全球布局。
- "Partnerships only: even if we spend the full 800 million dollars, sales reach only about 2.6 billion. That misses the goal." —— 只做合作：即使花光 8 亿美元，销售额也只有约 26 亿，达不到目标。
- "Buying in the US: sales grow, but 70 percent of sales would still follow the US egg price, and we would stay in one region." —— 在美国收购：销售额会涨，但 70% 的销售额仍然跟着美国鸡蛋价格走，而且我们还是只在一个地区。
- "Bargain hunting: a company in Syria looks cheap, but it is cheap because of war." —— 捡便宜：叙利亚的公司看起来便宜，但便宜是因为战争。
- "So we chose a mixed plan. It is the only one that passes all five tests." —— 所以我们选了组合方案，它是唯一五项检验全部通过的。

## 第 12 页 · 我们的计划和时间表
- "Now, the timing of our plan." —— 下面讲计划的时间安排。
- "In Year 1, we buy l'Acre in France while the seller is keen to sell, and we build the first factory." —— 第 1 年，趁卖家想卖，买下法国的 l'Acre，并建第一座工厂。
- "In Year 2, we buy S. Bound, our biggest target, and build the second factory." —— 第 2 年，买下我们最大的目标 S. Bound，建第二座工厂。
- "At the start of Year 3, we buy Planeta in Brazil and build the third factory." —— 第 3 年初，买下巴西的 Planeta，建第三座工厂。
- "The three companies cost about 311 million dollars. The factories and barns cost 85 million." —— 三家公司约 3.11 亿美元，工厂和鸡舍 8,500 万美元。
- "At the end of Year 2, we hold a check point." —— 第 2 年年底，我们设一个检查点。
- "If egg prices have fallen sharply, or a deal has failed, we buy Free Range Roost in Thailand early. If not, we buy it in Years 4 to 5." —— 如果蛋价大跌，或者某笔收购失败，就提前买泰国的 Free Range Roost；否则在第 4 到 5 年再买。
- "We keep 404 million dollars in reserve, so we can pay for it." —— 我们留着 4.04 亿美元储备，买得起它。
- "This copies what worked before: American Egg bought Cascade cheaply in a downturn." —— 这借鉴了以前成功的做法：American Egg 在低迷时低价买下了 Cascade。
- "So, what does this plan deliver?" —— 那么，这个计划能带来什么？

## 第 13 页 · 计划的结果
- "Our plan passes all five tests." —— 我们的计划五项检验全部通过。
- "First, sales reach 3.8 billion dollars in Year 3, about 9 percent above the goal." —— 第一，第 3 年销售额达到 38 亿美元，比目标高约 9%。
- "Second, premium eggs reach 33 percent of US sales." —— 第二，高端蛋占美国销售额的 33%。
- "Third, we are in all four regions, and 33 percent of sales come from outside North America." —— 第三，四个地区全部进入，33% 的销售额来自北美以外。
- "Fourth, we spend 396 million dollars, less than half of the 800 million budget." —— 第四，只花 3.96 亿美元，不到 8 亿预算的一半。
- "Fifth, the share of sales tied to the US egg price falls from 75 percent to 45 percent." —— 第五，跟着美国蛋价走的销售额占比，从 75% 降到 45%。
- "There is one trade-off, and we want to be honest about it." —— 有一个取舍，我们要坦白说。
- "Profit rises by 31 percent, to about 437 million dollars, but the profit margin falls from 14 to 11.5 percent." —— 利润增加 31%，达到约 4.37 亿美元，但利润率从 14% 降到 11.5%。
- "This is because the companies we buy earn less per dollar than American Egg." —— 因为我们收购的公司每一美元赚得比 American Egg 少。
- "We think this is worth it, for lower price risk and a global footprint." —— 我们认为值得，换来的是更低的价格风险和全球布局。
- "Now, my teammate will explain how we pay for the plan." —— 下面由我的组员讲我们怎么为这个计划出钱。
