# AmEgg 小组展示 · 演讲稿跟读（第四版，2026-10-21）
> 来自用户的演讲稿（`files/AmEgg_Speech_Script.docx`）。按页、按句拆开，在“跟读练习”里练。演讲稿改了要同步改这里，然后运行 `node scripts/build-practice.mjs` 和 `build-audio.py`。

## 第 1 页 · 封面
- "Good morning, everyone. We are a group of four students." —— 大家早上好，我们是一个四人小组。
- "Today we talk about American Egg. It is a family company in the US." —— 今天讲 American Egg，一家美国家族企业。
- "It sells eggs and makes 2.34 billion dollars a year." —— 它卖鸡蛋，年收入 23.4 亿美元。
- "The board wants three things. One: more than 3.5 billion dollars in three years." —— 董事会有三个目标。一：三年内收入超过 35 亿美元。
- "Two: more special eggs, at least 30 percent. Three: work in other countries within seven years." —— 二：更多特色鸡蛋，至少 30%。三：七年内进入其他国家。
- "Our idea is simple. Fix the product mix at home. Buy growth in other countries." —— 我们的思路很简单：国内调整产品结构，国外收购带来增长。

## 第 2 页 · 我们的分析思路
- "This slide shows our five steps. First, we define the problem." —— 这页是我们的五个步骤。第一，定义问题。
- "Second, we measure the numbers. Third, we look at the companies we can buy." —— 第二，计算数据。第三，筛选可以收购的公司。
- "Fourth, we read the text in the case and find themes. Fifth, we make a plan and test it." —— 第四，阅读案例文字、归纳主题。第五，制定方案并测试。
- "We use numbers, like averages and correlation. We also use words, like news and risks." —— 我们用数字，比如平均数和相关系数；也用文字，比如新闻和风险。
- "Each of us will present four slides." —— 我们每人讲四页。

## 第 3 页 · 业务问题
- "Here is the problem. Revenue is 2.34 billion dollars." —— 问题是这样的：收入是 23.4 亿美元。
- "Only 24.6 percent comes from special eggs." —— 只有 24.6% 来自特色鸡蛋。
- "Normal eggs make 12.4 percent profit. Special eggs make 20 percent." —— 普通鸡蛋利润率 12.4%，特色鸡蛋 20%。
- "Egg prices go up and down a lot. In one bad year, revenue fell 48 percent." —— 蛋价波动很大，有一个坏年份收入下跌了 48%。
- "So the company must grow fast, change its mix, and go global." —— 所以公司必须快速增长、调整结构、走向全球。
- "But it can only use 800 million dollars." —— 但它最多只能用 8 亿美元。

## 第 4 页 · 三个目标，三个缺口
- "We turned each goal into a number." —— 我们把每个目标都变成一个数字。
- "Goal one: from 2,337 million to 3,500 million dollars. Our plan reaches about 4,130 million in year three." —— 目标一：从 23.37 亿到 35 亿美元。我们的方案第三年达到约 41.3 亿。
- "Goal two: from 24.6 percent to 30 percent. Our plan reaches 34 percent." —— 目标二：从 24.6% 到 30%。我们的方案达到 34%。
- "Goal three: the case does not say what global means. So this is our own assumption." —— 目标三：案例没有说“全球化”是什么意思，所以这是我们自己的假设。
- "Four or more continents, and about half of revenue from new markets." —— 覆盖四个以上大洲，约一半收入来自新市场。
- "We go from two countries to eight. By year seven, 47 percent of revenue comes from new markets." —— 从两个国家扩展到八个。第七年，47% 的收入来自新市场。
- "Now I hand over to the next speaker." —— 下面交给下一位同学。

## 第 5 页 · 大宗蛋价是核心风险
- "Thank you. This chart shows egg prices last year." —— 谢谢。这张图是去年的蛋价。
- "The average price was 1.67 dollars a dozen. Prices changed a lot." —— 平均价格每打 1.67 美元，价格变化很大。
- "The coefficient of variation is about 21 percent. In one year, the price doubled." —— 变异系数约 21%，一年内价格翻了一倍。
- "The US Bureau of Labor Statistics says the average shop price hit a record of 4.95 dollars in January 2025." —— 美国劳工统计局说，2025 年 1 月平均零售价创下 4.95 美元的纪录。
- "In that month alone, bird flu cost about 18.8 million egg-laying hens." —— 仅那一个月，禽流感就造成约 1880 万只蛋鸡损失。
- "So normal eggs are risky. We need a plan that is safe, not just enough." —— 所以普通鸡蛋风险高。我们需要一个安全的方案，而不只是刚好达标。

## 第 6 页 · 国内举措对比
- "The case gives three ways to grow at home. We compare profit for each one million dollars." —— 案例给了三种国内增长方式。我们比较每投入一百万美元带来的利润。
- "The case says extra marketing gives a 6 percent upside." —— 案例说，额外营销能带来 6% 的增长空间。
- "This is our assumption: we read it as 6 percent more special egg sales." —— 这是我们的假设：我们把它理解为特色鸡蛋销售多 6%。
- "With that, marketing gives only 18 cents, because it costs money every year." —— 这样算，营销只带来 18 美分，因为它每年都要花钱。
- "Cage-free change gives 13 cents, but it is the cheapest way to change the mix." —— 散养改造带来 13 美分，但它是调整结构最便宜的办法。
- "A new egg product factory gives 40 cents. That is the best." —— 新建蛋制品工厂带来 40 美分，是最好的。
- "So we use factories for profit and cage-free for the mix." —— 所以我们用工厂赚利润，用散养调结构。

## 第 7 页 · 特色占比达到 34%
- "This chart shows how special eggs grow. We start at 576 million dollars." —— 这张图显示特色鸡蛋怎样增长。我们从 5.76 亿美元起步。
- "Marketing adds about 35 million. Market growth adds about 47 million." —— 营销增加约 3500 万，市场增长增加约 4700 万。
- "Two factories add 100 million. Cage-free change adds 100 million." —— 两座工厂增加 1 亿，散养改造增加 1 亿。
- "We reach about 858 million." —— 最后达到约 8.58 亿。
- "The share passes 30 percent in year two, and reaches 34 percent in year three." —— 占比第二年超过 30%，第三年达到 34%。
- "In November 2023, 38.8 percent of US hens were already cage-free, so the market is ready." —— 2023 年 11 月，美国已有 38.8% 的母鸡是散养的，所以市场已经准备好了。
- "Remember, the marketing number is our assumption." —— 记住，营销那个数字是我们的假设。

## 第 8 页 · 筛选 20 个国际目标
- "Now we look at 20 companies in other regions." —— 现在看其他地区的 20 家公司。
- "North American companies are big, but they are expensive." —— 北美公司规模大，但是贵。
- "They cost about 11 times their yearly profit. Asian companies cost only about 3 times." —— 价格约为年利润的 11 倍，亚洲公司只有约 3 倍。
- "The average company makes 504 million dollars." —— 平均每家公司收入 5.04 亿美元。
- "Almost all of them have the same profit margin, 5 percent." —— 几乎所有公司的利润率都一样，都是 5%。
- "So price, size and risk are what make them different. Now I hand over to the next speaker." —— 所以区别在于价格、规模和风险。下面交给下一位同学。

## 第 9 页 · 便宜的目标便宜是有原因的
- "Thank you. We gave each country a risk score from 1 to 5." —— 谢谢。我们给每个国家打了 1 到 5 分的风险分。
- "Then we compared risk with price. The correlation is minus 0.76." —— 然后把风险和价格做比较，相关系数是负 0.76。
- "This means: more risk, lower price." —— 意思是：风险越高，价格越低。
- "The cheapest companies are in Syria, Bangladesh and Ukraine. They have war, legal problems, or big losses." —— 最便宜的公司在叙利亚、孟加拉和乌克兰，它们有战争、法律问题或巨大损失。
- "So a low price is not good value. We only choose cheap companies if the risk is 3 or lower." —— 所以低价不等于划算。只有风险在 3 分或以下，我们才选便宜的公司。
- "But this result has limits. We have only 19 points. We gave the risk scores ourselves." —— 但这个结果有局限：只有 19 个数据点，风险分是我们自己打的。
- "And correlation does not prove cause. Size or data quality may also change the price." —— 而且相关不能证明因果，规模或数据质量也可能影响价格。

## 第 10 页 · 定性编码
- "We also read the words in the case. We put them into themes." —— 我们也读了案例里的文字，把它们归纳成主题。
- "Red bars are risks. Green bars are opportunities." —— 红色柱子是风险，绿色柱子是机会。
- "Price changes appear for seven companies. Animal disease appears for six." —— 价格波动出现在七家公司，动物疫病出现在六家。
- "So keeping birds healthy is very important." —— 所以保持禽类健康非常重要。
- "Five companies are moving to cage-free eggs. This fits our goal." —— 五家公司正在转向散养鸡蛋，这符合我们的目标。
- "We also want partners who fit our company culture, because we treat workers well." —— 我们也希望合作方符合我们的公司文化，因为我们善待员工。

## 第 11 页 · 加权评分模型
- "Here we put numbers and words together in one score." —— 这里我们把数字和文字合成一个总分。
- "Country risk has the biggest weight, 25 percent." —— 国家风险的权重最大，25%。
- "Canyon Crossing has the top score, but it costs more than one billion dollars. That is too much." —— Canyon Crossing 分数最高，但价格超过 10 亿美元，太贵了。
- "It is also our biggest US competitor. So we remove it." —— 它也是我们在美国最大的竞争对手，所以我们把它排除。
- "We choose companies in Japan and India, Thailand, Brazil, Mexico and France. We also keep two backups." —— 我们选择日本和印度、泰国、巴西、墨西哥和法国的公司，另外留两个备选。

## 第 12 页 · 建议与收购路线
- "This is our plan. At home, we build two factories and change some farms to cage-free." —— 这是我们的方案。国内建两座工厂，把部分农场改成散养。
- "Abroad, we buy one company each year." —— 国外每年收购一家公司。
- "Year one: Japan and India. Year two: Thailand. Year three: Brazil." —— 第一年日本和印度，第二年泰国，第三年巴西。
- "Year four: Mexico. Year five: France." —— 第四年墨西哥，第五年法国。
- "The first three add 1.6 billion dollars. One deal a year is easier for our managers." —— 前三家增加 16 亿美元收入。每年一笔交易，管理层更容易应对。
- "Now I hand over to the next speaker." —— 下面交给下一位同学。

## 第 13 页 · 资金安排
- "Thank you. Now the money. We can use 800 million dollars." —— 谢谢。现在讲资金。我们可以用 8 亿美元。
- "We use 658 million. We keep 142 million for safety." —— 我们用 6.58 亿，留 1.42 亿作为安全储备。
- "We use our own 300 million first. Then we borrow 358 million at 3 percent." —— 先用自己的 3 亿，再按 3% 的利率借 3.58 亿。
- "The interest is about 11 million a year." —— 利息每年约 1100 万美元。
- "The new companies make about 114 million a year. So we can pay the interest more than ten times." —— 新公司每年利润约 1.14 亿，所以能覆盖利息十倍以上。

## 第 14 页 · 七年收入路径
- "This chart shows revenue for seven years. The dashed line is the 3.5 billion dollar target." —— 这张图是七年的收入，虚线是 35 亿美元的目标。
- "Grey is normal eggs. Yellow is special eggs. Green is other countries." —— 灰色是普通鸡蛋，黄色是特色鸡蛋，绿色是海外。
- "We pass the target in year two. In year three, we reach about 4.13 billion." —— 第二年就超过目标，第三年达到约 41.3 亿。
- "In year seven, about 4.9 billion. Almost half comes from other countries." —— 第七年约 49 亿，接近一半来自海外。
- "We did not add any growth for the new companies, so this is a safe number." —— 我们没有给新公司加任何增长，所以这是一个保守的数字。

## 第 15 页 · 压力测试
- "Egg prices can fall, so we tested this." —— 蛋价可能下跌，所以我们做了测试。
- "If prices fall 10, 20 or 30 percent, we still pass 3.5 billion." —— 如果价格下跌 10%、20% 或 30%，我们仍然超过 35 亿。
- "We only fail if prices fall more than 38 percent." —— 只有跌幅超过 38% 才不达标。
- "If a big fall like 48 percent happens again, we buy the Mexican company one year early." —— 如果再出现 48% 这样的大跌，我们就提前一年收购墨西哥公司。
- "Then revenue goes back to about 3.7 billion." —— 这样收入回到约 37 亿。
- "Also, when prices fall, special eggs become a bigger share." —— 另外，价格下跌时，特色鸡蛋的占比反而变大。

## 第 16 页 · 目标 1 的措施与风险
- "For each goal, the case asks for two objectives and two risks." —— 案例要求每个目标写两个措施和两个风险。
- "For goal one, our first objective is to buy three companies by year three." —— 目标一的第一个措施是三年内收购三家公司。
- "Risks: prices can crash, and we can pay too much. We bid low and keep local leaders." —— 风险：价格可能暴跌，我们可能出价过高。应对：压低报价，保留当地管理层。
- "Our second objective is to grow at home." —— 第二个措施是国内增长。
- "Risks: bird flu, which cost about 18.8 million US hens in January 2025 alone, and factory delays." —— 风险：禽流感（仅 2025 年 1 月就让美国损失约 1880 万只蛋鸡）和工厂延期。
- "We use strong bird safety and sell factory products early. Now I hand over to the next speaker." —— 应对：加强禽类安全，提前预售工厂产品。下面交给下一位同学。

## 第 17 页 · 目标 2 的措施与风险
- "Thank you. Goal two is about special eggs. First, cage-free change." —— 谢谢。目标二是特色鸡蛋。措施一是散养改造。
- "Risk: many farms are changing. In November 2023, 38.8 percent of US hens were already cage-free." —— 风险：很多农场都在改。2023 年 11 月，美国已有 38.8% 的母鸡是散养的。
- "So prices for cage-free eggs may fall. We only change farms when customers sign a contract." —— 所以散养蛋价可能下降。我们只在客户签了合同后才改造农场。
- "Second, factories and marketing. Risk: people may buy fewer ready-made egg products." —— 措施二是工厂和营销。风险：人们可能少买加工好的蛋制品。
- "We sell to many types of customers. Marketing gives little profit, so we check it after 12 months." —— 我们卖给多种客户。营销利润少，所以 12 个月后检查效果。

## 第 18 页 · 目标 3 的措施与风险
- "Goal three is about going global. First, we buy companies on four continents." —— 目标三是走向全球。措施一是在四大洲收购公司。
- "Risks: politics and exchange rates can change, and cultures are different." —— 风险：政治和汇率会变化，文化也不同。
- "We borrow some money in local currency, and we keep local managers." —— 我们部分借款用当地货币，并保留当地管理层。
- "Second, we keep our money safe. Risks: lower profit, or a company is sold to someone else." —— 措施二是保证资金安全。风险：利润下降，或者目标公司被别人买走。
- "We use a fixed 3 percent rate and keep a backup list." —— 我们锁定 3% 的固定利率，并准备备选名单。

## 第 19 页 · 协同与冲突
- "Our goals can help each other. Each new company helps goal one and goal three." —— 我们的目标可以互相帮助。每收购一家新公司，同时帮助目标一和目标三。
- "Factories help the mix and the profit." —— 工厂既改善产品结构，又提高利润。
- "But goals can also fight. Buying a big US company is fast, but it lowers the special egg share." —— 但目标之间也会冲突。收购美国大公司很快，但会拉低特色鸡蛋的占比。
- "Money for factories means less money for deals." —— 建工厂的钱多了，收购的钱就少了。
- "And our managers have limited time. So we do one deal a year." —— 而且管理层的时间有限，所以我们每年只做一笔交易。

## 第 20 页 · 结论
- "To finish, here are our three points." —— 最后，我们的三个要点。
- "One: we can meet all three goals with 658 million dollars." —— 一：用 6.58 亿美元就能实现全部三个目标。
- "Two: a low price is not always good value. Risk matters." —— 二：低价不一定划算，风险很重要。
- "Three: egg prices change a lot, so our plan has a safety buffer." —— 三：蛋价变化很大，所以我们的方案留有安全余量。
- "Our last slide lists all our sources." —— 最后一页列出了我们所有的资料来源。

- "Here are our references, in Harvard style." —— 这是我们的参考文献，用的是 Harvard 格式。
- "We used the Sage case, and three outside sources for egg prices, bird flu and cage-free hens." —— 我们用了 Sage 案例，以及关于蛋价、禽流感和散养母鸡的三个外部来源。
