## 第 1 页 · FDI 理论
- "Thank you. My theory is FDI, foreign direct investment." —— 谢谢。我讲的理论是 FDI，外国直接投资。
- "FDI means a company owns and controls a business in another country." —— FDI 是指一家公司在另一个国家拥有并控制一项业务。
- "There are two ways: greenfield, which means building something new, and M&A, which means buying a company." —— 有两种方式：绿地投资，就是自己新建；还有并购，就是买下一家公司。
- "Dunning's OLI model explains why companies do FDI." —— Dunning 的 OLI 模型解释了公司为什么做 FDI。
- "O is ownership advantage, like brands. L is location advantage, like a big market or low costs. I is internalisation: doing it yourself is better than licensing." —— O 是所有权优势，比如品牌；L 是区位优势，比如市场大或成本低；I 是内部化优势：自己做比授权给别人更好。
- "There are four motives: market-seeking, resource-seeking, efficiency-seeking and strategic asset-seeking." —— FDI 有四种动机：寻求市场、寻求资源、寻求效率和寻求战略资产。

## 第 2 页 · Unilever 的 FDI ①：建和买
- "Now, Unilever. First, building new: greenfield." —— 现在看 Unilever。第一，自己新建：绿地投资。
- "In June 2020, Unilever invested 100 million euros in its ice cream factory in Taicang, China." —— 2020 年 6 月，Unilever 投资 1 亿欧元扩建它在中国太仓的冰淇淋工厂。
- "The new factory opened in April 2022. This is market-seeking." —— 新工厂 2022 年 4 月启用。这是寻求市场。
- "In February 2023, Unilever announced a new factory in Mexico, and in April 2025 it doubled the investment to 800 million dollars." —— 2023 年 2 月，Unilever 宣布在墨西哥建新工厂，2025 年 4 月把投资翻倍到 8 亿美元。
- "It will create 850 jobs and mainly export to the US and Canada. This is market-seeking and efficiency-seeking." —— 它会创造 850 个工作岗位，主要出口到美国和加拿大。这既是寻求市场，也是寻求效率。
- "Second, buying: M&A." —— 第二，买：并购。
- "In August 2021, Unilever bought Paula's Choice, an American skincare brand, for 1.8 billion euros. In 2022, it helped Prestige Beauty grow by double digits." —— 2021 年 8 月，Unilever 花 18 亿欧元买下美国护肤品牌 Paula's Choice。2022 年，它帮助高端美妆业务实现两位数增长。
- "In September 2025, it bought Dr. Squatch for 1.2 billion euros, to move into premium, high-growth men's care." —— 2025 年 9 月，它花 12 亿欧元买下 Dr. Squatch，进入高端、高增长的男士个护市场。
- "These deals are strategic asset-seeking: Unilever buys brands and online skills." —— 这些交易是寻求战略资产：Unilever 买的是品牌和网上销售的能力。
- "But there is risk: when the US premium beauty market slowed in 2024, Paula's Choice slowed too." —— 但也有风险：2024 年美国高端美妆市场放缓，Paula's Choice 也跟着放缓了。

## 第 3 页 · Unilever 的 FDI ②：卖
- "Third, Unilever also sells." —— 第三，Unilever 也在卖。
- "In July 2022, it sold its tea business for 4.6 billion euros. It made a gain of 2.3 billion euros and reduced its debt, but its turnover in 2023 was 1.7 percent lower." —— 2022 年 7 月，它以 46 亿欧元卖掉茶叶业务，赚了 23 亿欧元，债务也减少了，但 2023 年的营业额因此少了 1.7%。
- "In October 2024, it sold its Russian business. Together with three other sales that year, this gave a net loss of 406 million euros." —— 2024 年 10 月，它卖掉了俄罗斯业务。连同当年另外三笔出售，净亏损 4.06 亿欧元。
- "In December 2025, it demerged its ice cream business, Magnum. On the new basis, its 2024 turnover is 52.5 billion euros, not 60.8." —— 2025 年 12 月，它把冰淇淋业务 Magnum 分拆出去。按新的口径，它 2024 年的营业额是 525 亿欧元，而不是 608 亿。
- "But the demerger gave a gain of 3.4 billion euros, and Unilever kept 19.85 percent of Magnum." —— 但分拆带来 34 亿欧元的收益，Unilever 还保留了 Magnum 19.85% 的股份。
- "My conclusion: Unilever's FDI follows the OLI model. Its ownership advantage is its brands." —— 我的结论：Unilever 的 FDI 符合 OLI 模型，它的所有权优势是品牌。
- "It builds in big markets, buys beauty brands, and sells slower businesses. So it became smaller, but more focused." —— 它在大市场建厂、买美妆品牌、卖掉增长慢的业务，所以变小了，但更聚焦了。

## 第 4 页 · 小组结论
- "To conclude: Unilever is global, it works through regions, it uses its advantages as a British MNE, and it grows through FDI." —— 总结一下：Unilever 是全球化的公司，按地区经营，利用它作为英国跨国企业的优势，并通过 FDI 发展。

## 答问 1 · 为什么 Unilever 买公司，而不是自己做新品牌？
- "Why does Unilever buy companies instead of building new brands?" —— 问：为什么 Unilever 买公司，而不是自己做新品牌？
- "Because buying is faster." —— 答：因为买更快。
- "A brand like Paula's Choice already has customers and online sales skills. This is strategic asset-seeking." —— 像 Paula's Choice 这样的品牌已经有客户，也有网上销售的能力，这就是寻求战略资产。
- "Building a new brand takes many years and may fail." —— 自己做一个新品牌要很多年，还可能失败。
- "But buying has risks too: for example, when the US premium beauty market slowed in 2024, Paula's Choice slowed too." —— 但买也有风险：比如 2024 年美国高端美妆市场放缓，Paula's Choice 也跟着放缓了。

## 答问 2 · FDI 对 Unilever 和东道国各有什么风险？
- "What are the risks of FDI, for Unilever and for the host country?" —— 问：FDI 对 Unilever 和东道国各有什么风险？
- "For Unilever, there is political risk. For example, it stayed in Russia until October 2024, and the sales that year gave a net loss." —— 答：对 Unilever 来说，有政治风险。比如它在俄罗斯一直待到 2024 年 10 月，当年的出售带来了净亏损。
- "There is also currency risk, because it earns money in many currencies." —— 还有汇率风险，因为它用很多种货币赚钱。
- "For the host country, FDI brings jobs. The Mexico factory is expected to create 850 direct jobs." —— 对东道国来说，FDI 带来就业：墨西哥工厂预计创造 850 个直接就业岗位。
- "But profits can leave the country, and local companies may find it hard to compete." —— 但利润可能流出这个国家，当地企业也可能很难竞争。
