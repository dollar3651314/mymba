## 第 1 页 · 什么是 M&A（引言）
- "Thank you. My part is M&A, mergers and acquisitions." —— 谢谢。我讲的是 M&A，也就是合并与收购。
- "In an acquisition, one company buys another. In a merger, two similar companies join into one." —— 收购是一家公司买下另一家；合并是两家规模相近的公司合成一家。
- "The goal is synergy: one plus one is more than two." —— 目标是协同效应：一加一大于二。
- "But 70 to 90 percent of deals fail, mostly because joining two companies is hard." —— 但 70% 到 90% 的并购会失败，主要因为两家公司很难整合。
- "Also, buying a company in another country is a kind of FDI." —— 另外，在另一个国家收购公司，也是一种 FDI。

## 第 2 页 · 2020–2026：买美妆、卖食品
- "Our group looks at 2020 to 2026. In this time, Unilever buys beauty and personal care brands, and sells food." —— 我们组分析 2020 到 2026 年。这段时间，Unilever 买美妆和个护品牌，卖食品。
- "In 2021, it bought Paula's Choice for about 1.8 billion euros." —— 2021 年，它花约 18 亿欧元买下 Paula's Choice。
- "In 2025, it bought Dr. Squatch for about 1.5 billion dollars." —— 2025 年，它花约 15 亿美元买下 Dr. Squatch（媒体报道的金额）。
- "In 2022, it tried to buy GSK's consumer health business, but the offer failed and its shareholders did not like it." —— 2022 年，它想买 GSK 的消费者健康业务，但没有成功，它的股东也不喜欢这个计划。
- "Then Unilever started to sell." —— 然后 Unilever 开始卖。
- "In 2023, it sold most of Dollar Shave Club, because online selling did not fit its supermarket business." —— 2023 年，它卖掉了 Dollar Shave Club 的大部分，因为网上直销和它的超市生意不搭。
- "In 2025, it spun off ice cream. In 2026, it agreed to combine its food business with McCormick." —— 2025 年，它分拆了冰淇淋业务。2026 年，它同意把食品业务和 McCormick 合并。

## 第 3 页 · 用并购理论看 McCormick 交易
- "Why sell food? Food grows slowly, only 2.5 percent in 2025." —— 为什么卖食品？食品增长慢，2025 年只增长 2.5%。
- "A spin-off can release value, and Unilever gets 15.7 billion dollars to buy beauty brands." —— 分拆可以释放价值，Unilever 还能拿到 157 亿美元去买美妆品牌。
- "This deal is horizontal, because both companies sell sauces and seasonings." —— 这是横向并购，因为两家都卖酱料和调味品。
- "The synergy is about 300 million dollars a year." —— 协同效应是每年约 3 亿美元。
- "The risks: joining people is hard, regulators must agree, and the deal will close only in 2027." —— 风险是：人员整合难，要监管机构批准，而且交易 2027 年才完成。
- "My conclusion: overall, M&A is a success for Unilever. It helps Unilever grow and focus." —— 我的结论：总体来说，并购对 Unilever 是成功的，它帮 Unilever 做大，也帮它聚焦。
- "But deals with no synergy, like Dollar Shave Club, failed." —— 但没有协同效应的收购，比如 Dollar Shave Club，失败了。

## 答问 1 · 为什么失败率高还要做并购
- "Why do companies still do M&A if 70 to 90 percent fail?" —— 问：既然 70% 到 90% 的并购会失败，公司为什么还要做？
- "It is fast. A company can get new brands, new customers and new markets quickly." —— 答：速度快，能很快得到新品牌、新客户和新市场。
- "Some managers also want to make the company bigger." —— 也有一些经理想把公司做大。

## 答问 2 · 为什么买 Paula's Choice 和 Dr. Squatch
- "Why does Unilever buy brands like Paula's Choice and Dr. Squatch?" —— 问：Unilever 为什么买 Paula's Choice 和 Dr. Squatch 这样的品牌？
- "They are premium brands that grow fast. Paula's Choice is strong online." —— 答：它们是增长快的高端品牌，Paula's Choice 在网上很强。
- "This fits Unilever's plan to focus on beauty and personal care." —— 这符合 Unilever 聚焦美妆和个护的计划。

## 答问 3 · 卖掉食品业务是好事吗
- "Is selling the food business good for Unilever?" —— 问：卖掉食品业务对 Unilever 是好事吗？
- "I think yes. Food grows slowly, and the cash can buy faster-growing beauty brands." —— 答：我认为是。食品增长慢，现金可以去买增长更快的美妆品牌。
- "The risk is that the deal is not finished until 2027, and regulators must agree." —— 风险是交易 2027 年才完成，而且要监管机构批准。
