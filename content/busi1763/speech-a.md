## 第 1 页 · FDI 理论
- "Thank you. My theory is FDI, foreign direct investment, and my country is the United States." —— 谢谢。我讲的理论是 FDI，外国直接投资，我选的国家是美国。
- "FDI means a company owns and controls a business in another country. It can build something new, which is greenfield, or buy a company, which is M&A." —— FDI 是指一家公司在另一个国家拥有并控制一项业务。可以自己新建，叫绿地投资；也可以买下一家公司，叫并购。
- "Dunning's OLI model explains why companies do FDI: ownership advantages, location advantages and internalisation advantages." —— Dunning 的 OLI 模型解释了公司为什么做 FDI：所有权优势、区位优势和内部化优势。
- "There are four motives: market-seeking, resource-seeking, efficiency-seeking and strategic asset-seeking." —— FDI 有四种动机：寻求市场、寻求资源、寻求效率和寻求战略资产。

## 第 2 页 · 为什么是美国：OLI
- "Why the United States? The US is the world's largest FDI host. From January to December 2024, it received 279 billion dollars." —— 为什么是美国？美国是全球最大的 FDI 接收国，2024 年 1–12 月吸收了 2,790 亿美元。
- "For Unilever, the US is its biggest market. Its US turnover grew from 9.4 billion euros in the year to December 2020 to 12.5 billion in the year to December 2024, about one fifth of the group." —— 对 Unilever 来说，美国是它最大的市场。它在美国的营业额从 2020 年 1–12 月的 94 亿欧元，增长到 2024 年 1–12 月的 125 亿欧元，约占集团的五分之一。
- "Using OLI: O, Unilever has global brands, money and distribution. L, the US is the biggest market, with many premium and online shoppers." —— 用 OLI 来看：O，Unilever 有全球品牌、资金和销售网络；L，美国是最大的市场，有很多愿意买高端产品、在网上购物的消费者。
- "I, Unilever buys and owns these brands, instead of licensing them, so it controls the brands, their customers and their know-how." —— I，Unilever 买下并拥有这些品牌，而不是授权给别人，所以它能控制品牌、客户和专业知识。

## 第 3 页 · Unilever 在美国的并购和好处
- "Unilever's FDI in the US was mainly M&A. This is strategic asset-seeking: it buys brands, customers and online skills." —— Unilever 在美国的 FDI 主要是并购。这是寻求战略资产：它买的是品牌、客户和网上销售的能力。
- "In August 2021, it bought Paula's Choice, a skincare brand that sells online, for 1.8 billion euros. In the year to December 2022, Prestige Beauty grew by double digits." —— 2021 年 8 月，它花 18 亿欧元买下网上直销的护肤品牌 Paula's Choice。2022 年 1–12 月，高端美妆业务实现两位数增长。
- "In February 2024, it bought K18, which grew by double digits by December 2024. In September 2025, it bought Dr. Squatch for 1.2 billion euros." —— 2024 年 2 月，它买下 K18，到 2024 年 12 月实现两位数增长。2025 年 9 月，它花 12 亿欧元买下 Dr. Squatch。
- "What are the benefits for Unilever? First, faster growth: by December 2024, 70 percent of its US Beauty and Wellbeing sales came from these premium brands." —— 给 Unilever 带来什么好处？第一，增长更快：到 2024 年 12 月，它在美国美妆和健康业务 70% 的销售来自这些高端品牌。
- "Second, a stronger position: its long-term assets in the US grew from 12.9 billion euros in December 2020 to 19.7 billion in December 2024." —— 第二，地位更稳：它在美国的长期资产从 2020 年 12 月的 129 亿欧元增长到 2024 年 12 月的 197 亿欧元。
- "Third, results: in the year to December 2025, North America grew 5.3 percent, and Unilever recovered much of its lost market share in the US." —— 第三，成果：2025 年 1–12 月，北美增长 5.3%，Unilever 在美国夺回了大部分失去的市场份额。
- "But there is risk: from January to December 2024, the US premium beauty market slowed, and Paula's Choice slowed too." —— 但也有风险：2024 年 1–12 月，美国高端美妆市场放缓，Paula's Choice 也跟着放缓了。
- "My conclusion: Unilever's FDI in the US follows the OLI model. By buying American premium brands, it gained growth, online skills and a stronger position in its biggest market." —— 我的结论：Unilever 在美国的 FDI 符合 OLI 模型。通过买美国的高端品牌，它获得了增长、网上销售能力，也巩固了在最大市场的地位。

## 第 4 页 · 小组结论
- "To conclude: Unilever is global, it uses trade deals like USMCA, it uses its British advantages, and it grows through FDI in the US." —— 总结一下：Unilever 是全球化的公司，利用美墨加协定这样的贸易协定，利用它作为英国企业的优势，并通过在美国的 FDI 发展。
- "Thank you for listening. We are happy to take your questions." —— 谢谢大家。欢迎提问。

## 答问 1 · 为什么 Unilever 买美国公司，而不是在美国自己做新品牌或建新工厂？
- "Why does Unilever buy American companies instead of building new brands or factories in the US?" —— 问：为什么 Unilever 买美国公司，而不是在美国自己做新品牌或建新工厂？
- "Because buying is faster." —— 答：因为买更快。
- "A brand like Paula's Choice already has customers and online sales skills. This is strategic asset-seeking." —— 像 Paula's Choice 这样的品牌已经有客户，也有网上销售的能力，这就是寻求战略资产。
- "The US market is very competitive, so building a new brand takes many years and may fail." —— 美国市场竞争很激烈，自己做一个新品牌要很多年，还可能失败。
- "But buying has risks too: it is expensive, and when the US premium beauty market slowed from January to December 2024, Paula's Choice slowed too." —— 但买也有风险：很贵，而且 2024 年 1–12 月美国高端美妆市场放缓时，Paula's Choice 也跟着放缓了。

## 答问 2 · 美国从 Unilever 的 FDI 中得到什么？Unilever 又有什么风险？
- "What does the United States gain from Unilever's FDI, and what are the risks for Unilever?" —— 问：美国从 Unilever 的 FDI 中得到什么？Unilever 又有什么风险？
- "The US gains investment and jobs, and the brands can grow faster with Unilever's money and its global network." —— 答：美国得到投资和就业，这些品牌也能借助 Unilever 的资金和全球网络发展得更快。
- "For example, after July 2022, Nutrafol grew quickly with Unilever's support, and it led double-digit growth in Wellbeing in the year to December 2025." —— 比如 2022 年 7 月以后，Nutrafol 在 Unilever 的支持下发展很快，2025 年 1–12 月带动健康业务实现两位数增长。
- "For Unilever, the risks are paying too much, a slowdown in the US market, and US trade policy, such as new tariffs." —— 对 Unilever 来说，风险是出价太高、美国市场放缓，还有美国的贸易政策，比如新关税。
