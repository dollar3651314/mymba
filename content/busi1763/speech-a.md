## 第 1 页 · FDI 理论
- "Thank you. My teammate showed that the US is Unilever's biggest market. My theory, FDI, explains how Unilever invests there to grow." —— 谢谢。上一位组员讲了美国是 Unilever 最大的市场。我的理论 FDI，解释 Unilever 怎样在美国投资来增长。
- "FDI means a company owns and controls a business in another country." —— FDI 是指一家公司在另一个国家拥有并控制一项业务。
- "It can build a new business. This is called greenfield investment." —— 它可以新建一项业务，这叫绿地投资。
- "Or it can buy a company. This is called M&A." —— 也可以收购一家公司，这叫并购。
- "Dunning's OLI model explains why companies do this." —— Dunning 的 OLI 模型解释了公司为什么这样做。
- "O is ownership: the company's own strengths, such as brands." —— O 是所有权优势：公司自己的长处，比如品牌。
- "L is location: the benefits of the host country." —— L 是区位优势：东道国的好处。
- "I is internalisation: the company owns the business, and does not license it to others." —— I 是内部化：公司自己拥有这项业务，而不是授权给别人。
- "Companies invest abroad to find customers, resources, lower costs, or strategic assets." —— 公司到海外投资，是为了找客户、资源、更低的成本或战略资产。
- "For Unilever in the US, strategic assets are the most important." —— 对 Unilever 在美国来说，战略资产最重要。

## 第 2 页 · 为什么是美国：OLI
- "Why the US? In 2024, the US received almost one fifth of all FDI in the world, more than any other country." —— 为什么是美国？2024 年美国吸引了全世界将近五分之一的外国直接投资，比任何国家都多。
- "It is also Unilever's biggest market, with about 21 percent of turnover in 2025." —— 它也是 Unilever 最大的市场，2025 年约占营业额的 21%。
- "The OLI model fits this case." —— OLI 模型正好适用于这个案例。
- "O: Unilever has strong brands, financial resources, and distribution networks." —— O：Unilever 有强大的品牌、财务资源和销售网络。
- "L: the US has high purchasing power and many premium and online shoppers." —— L：美国购买力强，有很多买高端产品和网购的消费者。
- "I: Unilever buys and owns the brands, so it controls them." —— I：Unilever 收购并拥有这些品牌，所以能控制它们。

## 第 3 页 · Unilever 在美国的并购和好处
- "Unilever grew in the US mainly by M&A, not by greenfield." —— Unilever 在美国主要靠并购增长，而不是绿地投资。
- "In August 2021, it bought Paula's Choice, an online skincare brand, for about 2.2 billion dollars." —— 2021 年 8 月，它花约 22 亿美元收购了网上护肤品牌 Paula's Choice。
- "In July 2022, it bought another 67 percent of Nutrafol, a hair wellness brand, for about 0.85 billion dollars. This raised its stake to 80 percent." —— 2022 年 7 月，它花约 8.5 亿美元再买入护发健康品牌 Nutrafol 67% 的股份，持股提高到 80%。
- "In February 2024, it bought K18, a hair care brand. The price was not disclosed." —— 2024 年 2 月，它收购了护发品牌 K18，价格没有公开。
- "In September 2025, it bought Dr. Squatch, a men's personal care brand, for about 1.4 billion dollars." —— 2025 年 9 月，它花约 14 亿美元收购了男士个人护理品牌 Dr. Squatch。
- "These dollar values are converted from the euro amounts in Unilever's annual reports, so they are estimates." —— 这些美元金额是从 Unilever 年报里的欧元金额换算来的，所以是估计值。
- "So what are the benefits for Unilever?" —— 那么，对 Unilever 有什么好处？
- "First, it gets strong brands and customers at once." —— 第一，它一下子就得到了强大的品牌和客户。
- "Second, it gets online selling skills." —— 第二，它得到了网上销售的能力。
- "By December 2024, premium brands made 70 percent of its US beauty and wellbeing sales." —— 到 2024 年 12 月，高端品牌占它美国美妆和健康业务销售额的 70%。
- "And in 2025, its sales in North America grew by 5.3 percent, faster than the 3.5 percent of the whole company." —— 2025 年，它在北美的销售额增长 5.3%，比整个公司的 3.5% 更快。
- "But there are risks. Buying companies is expensive, and strong brands do not always keep growing." —— 但也有风险：收购很贵，强势品牌也不一定一直增长。
- "In 2024, the US premium beauty market slowed, and Paula's Choice slowed too." —— 2024 年美国高端美妆市场放缓，Paula's Choice 也跟着放缓。
- "My conclusion: Unilever's US strategy fits Dunning's OLI model, and M&A is faster than greenfield, but it is not risk-free." —— 我的结论：Unilever 的美国策略符合 Dunning 的 OLI 模型；并购比绿地投资快，但并不是没有风险。
- "But what does Unilever bring to the US as a British MNE, and where do its goals clash with the host country? My teammate will explain MNEs from advanced economies." —— 但作为一家英国跨国企业，Unilever 给美国带来了什么？它的目标又和东道国在哪里冲突？下面由组员讲发达经济体的跨国企业。

## 答问 1 · 为什么 Unilever 买美国公司，而不是自己建新品牌？
- "Why does Unilever buy American companies, and not build new brands?" —— 问：Unilever 为什么收购美国公司，而不是自己建新品牌？
- "Buying is faster. The company already has customers, a good name, and useful skills." —— 答：收购更快。被收购的公司已经有客户、好名声和有用的能力。
- "For example, Paula's Choice gave Unilever a known skincare brand and online sales skills." —— 比如 Paula's Choice 给 Unilever 带来了一个知名护肤品牌和网上销售能力。
- "Building a new brand takes a long time, and it may fail." —— 自建新品牌要很长时间，而且可能失败。
- "But buying is expensive and still risky. Paula's Choice slowed down in 2024." —— 但收购很贵，也有风险：Paula's Choice 在 2024 年放缓了。

## 答问 2 · 美国从 Unilever 的 FDI 中得到什么？Unilever 有什么风险？
- "What does the US get from Unilever's FDI, and what are the risks for Unilever?" —— 问：美国从 Unilever 的 FDI 中得到什么？Unilever 有什么风险？
- "The US gets investment, jobs, and business growth." —— 答：美国得到投资、就业和商业增长。
- "The brands Unilever buys also get its financial resources and its global sales network." —— 被 Unilever 收购的品牌也能用上它的财务资源和全球销售网络。
- "For example, after July 2022, Unilever owned 80 percent of Nutrafol." —— 比如 2022 年 7 月以后，Unilever 持有 Nutrafol 80% 的股份。
- "For Unilever, the risks are paying too much, slower market growth, and changes in US trade policy, such as the tariffs of March 2025." —— 对 Unilever 来说，风险是买贵了、市场增长变慢，以及美国贸易政策变化，比如 2025 年 3 月的关税。
