**2. Opportunity Recognition**

The opportunity came from the founder's own experience. His dog is 12 years old, has moderate separation anxiety and a history of pancreatitis, gastritis and gallbladder problems. Yet he often travels for work, and no one can reliably look after it while he is away (founder interview, 2026). He realised that ordinary dog-walking and boarding services do not suit senior dogs that need stable companionship and special diets.

Following Zucchella, Hagen and Serapio (2018), this is a discovered opportunity: the demand already existed, and the founder simply noticed it earlier because of his own situation. Its risk can therefore be assessed through market research and customer feedback rather than being completely uncertain.

In 2024, China's urban dog and cat market reached about 300.2 billion yuan, up 7.5% on the previous year, yet services accounted for only 6.8% of spending (Table 1). Meanwhile, China had about 30 million dogs and cats aged seven or older in 2025. Senior pets are increasing, but care services remain small and underserved.

It also meets the three conditions of a viable opportunity:
- demand: young dog owners who live alone and travel often;
- feasibility: the company already has a home-visit team and partners with veterinary clinics and a fresh-food supplier;
- profitability: subscriptions bring stable monthly income and support sales of fresh food and related products.

Using Teece's (2007) framework of sensing, seizing and transforming, the company first tested demand through one-off home visits and built about 20 regular customers. The next step is to turn these services into a subscription plan.

### ✅ 第 1 节 · 英文（11/18 用户翻译，Claude 改语法、压缩到约 240 词）
**1. Business Idea**

Baby Pet Paradise is a home pet-care start-up founded in Shenzhen in 2025, with five employees. It is not a family business but a new venture that grew out of the founder's own experience. It currently offers home-visit dog walking, temporary feeding and pet boarding at 100 yuan per visit, and has about 20 regular customers (founder interview, 2026).

This report designs a new service for the company: the Baby Pet Home Care Plan, a monthly subscription for senior dogs. Subscribers receive:

- two to three visits a week from the same carer for companionship and walks;
- fresh food for senior dogs from a partner supplier;
- a health record (weight, diet and activity) after each visit, with alerts to the owner and bookings at a partner veterinary clinic when needed.

The target customers are young dog owners who live alone and travel often. As the founder explained, "time is their biggest cost, and they are willing to pay to save it". The value proposition is peace of mind: the dog is cared for by someone it knows while the owner is away.

Unlike existing one-off bookings, the plan offers a regular carer who knows each dog's habits and health. For the company, subscriptions also bring more stable revenue (see Section 4). As more young people live alone and their dogs grow older, the service meets a real and growing need (see Section 2). Figure 1 summarises the idea in a Business Model Canvas.

### 第 1 节 · 中文样板（Claude 11/18 起草，**参考用**：你确认 / 修改后自己翻译成英文，Claude 再改语法）
> 【】里的是 Claude 按访谈设想的细节，**请你和朋友核对**，不对就改。

**① 公司是谁**
Baby Pet Paradise（宠宝乐园）是 2025 年在深圳成立的一家宠物上门服务初创企业，目前有 5 名员工。它不是家族企业，而是创始人从自己的经历出发创办的新企业，目前主要提供上门遛狗、临时喂养和寄养服务，每次上门收费 100 元，约有 20 位固定客户（访谈，2026）。

**② 新服务是什么**
本报告为它设计一项新服务：Baby Pet Home Care Plan，一个专为老年犬设计的按月订阅照护计划。订阅客户每月可获得：【每周 2–3 次固定照护员上门陪伴和散步】、【适合老年犬的鲜食（由合作鲜食工厂提供）】、以及【每次上门记录的体重、饮食和活动情况，异常时提醒主人并可预约合作宠物医院】。价格将在第 5 节结合成本说明。

**③ 客户是谁、为什么买**
目标客户是经常出差、独自生活的年轻养狗人。他们工作压力大，却还要照顾越来越离不开人的老年犬。正如创始人所说：“时间是他们最大的成本，他们愿意花钱买时间。”订阅计划的价值主张，就是让主人不在家时，也能确定自己的老狗被熟悉的人好好照顾。

**④ 和现有服务有什么不同**
和“需要时才叫一次人”的遛狗、寄养相比，订阅计划由**固定的照护员**定期上门，熟悉每只狗的习惯和健康状况，并把陪伴、饮食和健康记录整合在一起。对公司来说，订阅也能带来**更稳定、可预测的收入**，帮助解决“客户少不赚钱、客户多人手不够”的问题（第 4 节再展开）。

**⑤ 为什么值得做**
城市里独居的年轻人越来越多，他们养的狗也在一起变老；而在中国的宠物消费中，服务只占很小一部分（第 2 节给出数据）。因此，这项服务既回应了真实的需求，也有成长空间。图 1 用商业模式画布总结这一想法。
